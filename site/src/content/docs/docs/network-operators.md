title: For network operators and rights holders
description: What traffic from the public relay addresses is, and where to write about it.
---

The addresses on [Relays](/docs/relays/), `79.127.204.69` and `152.233.49.59`, are shared outgoing addresses. Browser apps connect out from them through WebVPN and through FKN's HTTP proxy, so traffic from one of them comes from many people's apps at once. Both are run by Horion Corporation as part of FKN.

- **The relays do not monitor traffic,** and keep no log of the sites reached through them.
- **No record links a connection to a person.** The logs do not identify anyone, and usage is counted per day under a keyed hash and deleted after about 25 hours. See [what the relay keeps](/docs/what-the-relay-keeps/).
- **FKN's [terms](https://fkn.app/terms)** do not allow using the service to break the law, and make each person responsible for having the rights to what they reach through it.

What FKN's HTTP proxy keeps is in FKN's [privacy policy](https://fkn.app/privacy).

Write to [admin@fkn.app](mailto:admin@fkn.app).
